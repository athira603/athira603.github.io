2. Architectural Philosophy: 100% Offline-First

Unlike modern mobile applications that continuously stream telemetry, analytics, and personal profiles to external cloud infrastructures, Memory Flow operates completely independent of external application servers:

  • No User Accounts: You are not required to create an account, log in with an email, sign in via phone number, or authenticate via social logins (such as Google Sign-In, Apple, or Facebook).
  • No Remote Databases: We do not operate, license, or maintain remote databases, user directories, cloud synchronization services, or external backups.
  • No Tracking or Telemetry: There are no analytics trackers, crashlytics reporting daemons, session replay SDKs, or background pingers compiled into the production binary.
  • Deterministic Local Processing: All game mechanics, cognitive puzzles (Path Flow, Pulse Count, Pattern Recall), score calculations, and progression milestones are computed on-device in real-time.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

To provide total transparency under GDPR Article 13 and CCPA § 1798.100, we provide an explicit enumeration of data categories that are NEVER collected, stored, transmitted, or accessed by Memory Flow:

  • Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, usernames, physical addresses, postal codes, email addresses, phone numbers, or social media handles.
  • Hardware & Network Identifiers: We do not collect or log IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, Android Advertising ID (AAID), SIM card serials, or IP addresses.
  • Precise or Coarse Geolocation: We do not access GPS hardware, cell tower locations, or Wi-Fi triangulation data.
  • Biometric & Health Data: We do not collect fingerprint data, facial geometry, heartbeat, or biometric cognitive profiling.
  • Contacts & Communication: We do not access phone contacts, address books, SMS logs, call logs, or messaging records.
  • Camera & Audio: We do not access device cameras, photo galleries, or microphones. No audio or visual input is ever recorded.
  • Financial & Payment Credentials: We do not collect credit card numbers, debit cards, bank account details, or payment identifiers. All in-game shop items utilize virtual gameplay points ("Flux") with zero real-currency transactions.

4. Information Processed Locally On Your Device

To deliver a persistent and rewarding brain-training experience, Memory Flow stores limited operational game data exclusively within your device's isolated application sandbox:

4.1 Local Game Progress (Jetpack Room Database)

Using Android's native SQLite Room persistence library, the App writes the following data to your device's private storage:

  • Level Progression: Current level reached across game modes (Path Flow, Pulse Count, Pattern Recall).
  • Performance Metrics: High scores, completion times, accuracy percentages, and consecutive win streaks.
  • Player Progression: Accumulated in-game virtual "Flux" points, player rank titles, and unlocked achievement badges.

4.2 User Preferences (Jetpack DataStore)

The App stores your configuration preferences locally:

  • Audio Toggles: Sound effects enabled/disabled and ambient music enabled/disabled.
  • Haptic Toggle: Vibration feedback enabled/disabled.
  • Visual Aesthetic: The currently equipped UI theme identifier (e.g., Cyber Dark, Matrix Glow, Sunset Pulse).
  • Assist Mode: Toggle state for in-game hint assistance.
Sandboxed Isolation: Under Android's security architecture (Linux UID isolation & SELinux), this local database is encrypted by your device's file-based encryption (FBE) and cannot be accessed, read, or modified by other applications installed on your device.

8. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR / UK Data Protection Act)

Under the European General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the UK Data Protection Act 2018, organizations processing data must establish a lawful basis.

Because all data processed by Memory Flow is held exclusively on your own personal device, the legal grounds under Article 6(1) GDPR are:

  • Performance of a Contract (Article 6(1)(b)): Processing high scores, game progression, and visual settings is strictly necessary to fulfill your request to play and experience the cognitive exercises provided by the App.
  • Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)): Providing smooth gameplay animations, sound preferences, and haptic feedback configured by the user without compromising user privacy.

We do not engage in any processing that requires international data transfer of personal data from the EEA/UK to third countries, as all data remains strictly resident on your personal mobile hardware.

11. Data Storage, Security & Complete Deletion

11.1 Security Architecture

We rely on Google Android's multi-layered security model to protect all data stored on your device:

  • Kernel-Level Application Sandbox: Android assigns each installed app a unique Linux User ID (UID). Memory Flow's database, cache, and files are protected so no other application can access them.
  • Full Disk / File-Based Encryption (FBE): On all supported Android devices, data saved to internal storage is automatically encrypted at rest using hardware-backed cryptographic keys.

11.2 Data Retention Policy

Because all records exist purely on your physical device hardware, data retention is entirely governed by your choices:

  • Game scores, level milestones, and sound preferences persist on your device for as long as you maintain the application installed.
  • We do not retain backup archives, copies, or shadows on external servers.

11.3 Step-by-Step Self-Service Data Deletion

You possess instantaneous, autonomous control to erase 100% of your game data at any time without submitting a request or waiting for manual intervention. To permanently purge all Memory Flow data:

Method A: Reset All App Data
1. Open your Android device Settings.
2. Navigate to Apps > See all apps > Memory Flow.
3. Tap on Storage & cache.
4. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data) and confirm.
All progress, high scores, flux balance, and settings are instantly and permanently erased.
Method B: Complete Uninstall
1. Locate the Memory Flow app icon on your home screen or app drawer.
2. Long-press the icon and select Uninstall.
3. Confirm the prompt.
Android will purge the application and automatically wipe its private sandbox database and files.
