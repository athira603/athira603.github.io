2. Core Architecture & Data Minimization

Memory Flow is engineered using the principle of Data Minimization and Privacy by Design:

  • No User Account Mandatory: You can play Memory Flow without providing your name, email address, phone number, or linking social media accounts.
  • Local-First Cognitive Engine: All puzzle algorithms (Path Flow, Pulse Count, Pattern Recall), score calculations, level progressions, and virtual flux points are computed and retained locally within your device's isolated application storage sandbox.
  • Secure Integration with Certified Partners: When connecting to the internet to serve ads or execute one-time purchases, Memory Flow relies exclusively on industry-standard Google Play Services, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), and Google Play Billing APIs.

3. Information We Collect & Process

3.1 Information Stored Locally On Your Device (Not Transmitted to Our Servers)

The App generates and stores operational game data exclusively within the Android private storage sandbox:

  • Game Progression: Level completed, highest streak, completion speed, accuracy percentage.
  • In-Game Rewards: Accumulated virtual "Flux" points and achievement badges.
  • Preferences: Equipped visual theme ID, sound effect toggle, ambient music toggle, haptic feedback toggle, and assist mode state.

3.2 Advertising & Device Information (Via Third-Party Ad Networks)

To provide free access, the App may display banner, interstitial, or rewarded video advertisements provided by Google AdMob and certified ad partners. When ads are requested or displayed, third-party advertising SDKs may automatically collect or process:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Android Advertising ID (AAID) (resettable by you via device settings).
  • Device & Hardware Specifications: Device model, manufacturer, screen resolution, operating system version, and system language.
  • Network & Connection Data: IP address (utilized by ad networks to determine coarse/approximate geographic location at country or city level for regional ad compliance and fraud prevention), network connection type (Wi-Fi, 4G, 5G).
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Information regarding ads displayed, impressions, video completion, clicks, and diagnostic telemetry to prevent fraud and bot traffic.

3.3 In-App Purchases & Transaction Information (Via Google Play Billing)

The App may offer optional one-time purchases (e.g., "Remove Ads", Lifetime Premium Access, Theme Bundles):

  • Financial Details Handled Solely by Google: All financial transactions, credit card numbers, billing addresses, and payment authentication are processed exclusively by Google Play In-App Billing (Google LLC) in compliance with PCI-DSS standards.
  • Developer Zero-Access Guarantee: The developer NEVER collects, receives, views, or stores your credit card numbers, bank accounts, or financial passwords.
  • Purchase Entitlement Records: The App only receives non-sensitive order tokens from the Google Play Billing API (such as Product ID, Purchase Token, Order ID, and Purchase Timestamp) to unlock premium features and permanently deactivate advertisements on your device.

3.4 Data We NEVER Collect

We do NOT collect, harvest, or request:

  • ❌ Real names, physical home addresses, or phone numbers
  • ❌ Phone contacts or address books
  • ❌ Precise GPS location data
  • ❌ Photos, media gallery, camera feed, or microphone audio
  • ❌ Biometric, facial recognition, or health data

5. Advertising Practices & Your Control Choices

5.1 Google AdMob & Third-Party Ad Partners

We partner with Google AdMob to serve advertisements. Google's use of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Technologies.

5.2 Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads

  • Personalized Ads: Delivered based on your interests, previous ad interactions, and device advertising identifiers (only served where legally permitted and where affirmative consent is obtained).
  • Non-Personalized Ads: Contextual ads based strictly on current app context, coarse location (country/city), and general parameters without tracking user history.

5.3 User Messaging Platform (UMP) & Consent in the EEA / UK

In compliance with European GDPR and ePrivacy directives, we implement Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP):

  • Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland are presented with a Consent Management dialog on first launch.
  • You have the right to consent to personalized ads, choose non-personalized ads, or configure individual vendor preferences.
  • You can revisit and revoke or modify your consent at any time via the in-game Settings menu.

5.4 Device-Level Advertising Controls (All Users)

You can control or disable personalized advertising across all Android apps directly from your device settings:

How to Reset or Delete Your Advertising ID on Android:
1. Open your device Settings.
2. Tap Google (or Privacy > Ads).
3. Tap Ads.
4. Tap Reset advertising ID (to disassociate past activity) or Delete advertising ID (to prevent apps from receiving your AAID for personalized ads).

5.5 Permanent Ad Removal (One-Time Purchase)

We provide a convenient option to permanently remove all third-party banner and interstitial advertisements from Memory Flow through a single, one-time purchase ("Remove Ads") via Google Play In-App Billing.

11. Data Storage, Security & Retention

11.1 Security Architecture

  • Transmission Security: All communications with Google Play Billing and Google AdMob servers take place over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3) protocols.
  • On-Device Encryption: Local Room SQLite database files and DataStore preferences are protected by Android's hardware-backed File-Based Encryption (FBE) and application sandboxing.

11.2 Data Retention Horizon

  • Local Game Records: Retained on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall Memory Flow.
  • Purchase Entitlements: Stored securely in your personal Google Account and queried via Google Play Billing API for the lifetime of your account.
  • Ad Network Logs: Retained by Google AdMob in accordance with Google's retention schedules (typically aggregated and anonymized after 9 to 14 months).

11.3 Step-by-Step Data Deletion

You have immediate self-service power to erase your local data at any time:

Method A: Clear Local Game Data
1. Open Android Settings > Apps > Memory Flow.
2. Tap Storage & cache.
3. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data).
All local scores, progress, and settings are wiped immediately.
Method B: Uninstall the App
Uninstalling Memory Flow automatically triggers the Android operating system to delete the private sandboxed database and preference files.
